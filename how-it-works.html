The Process

How PeakProCAI Works

A rigorous, double opt-in introduction process built for quality over speed.

01

Both Sides Apply

Investors and developers complete separate intake forms. We capture criteria in detail - asset class, check size, geography, return expectations, timeline, and accredited status.

02

We Qualify Both Sides

Every applicant goes through a qualification review and call. We confirm real capital, real deals, and real intent before anyone enters our network.

03

We Match Precisely

When a developer's raise aligns with an investor's exact criteria we identify the match. No partial fits. No assumptions.

04

We Confirm Interest Privately

Before anyone is connected - we approach the investor privately and confirm their interest in this specific deal. Then we approach the developer and confirm theirs.

05

We Make The Introduction

A warm email introduction is sent to both parties. Both are prepared, both are interested, and both know exactly who they are speaking with.

06

You Take It From There

Our job ends at the introduction. The relationship, the conversation, and the deal are yours.
