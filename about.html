About Ayomide

Ayomide Olaniyan founded PeakProCAI to solve a problem he identified clearly - the right capital and the right real estate deals exist in abundance around the world. What is missing is a trusted, rigorous bridge between them.

PeakProCAI operates as an independent capital introduction network - not a broker dealer, not a placement agent, not a fund. A connector. Qualified on both sides. Warm on every introduction.

Every introduction Ayomide makes is one he is prepared to put his name behind. That selectivity is not a limitation - it is the product.

The Philosophy

What Drives This

Quality Over Volume

One right introduction is worth more than ten wrong ones. We take the time to get it right.

Both Sides Win

We only make introductions where we believe there is genuine value for both parties. Our reputation depends on it.

Trust Is The Product

Investors trust us with their criteria. Developers trust us with their raises. We protect both with the same level of care and discretion.
